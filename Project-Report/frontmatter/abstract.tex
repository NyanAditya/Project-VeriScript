\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}
\begin{spacing}{1.9}
Large language models now write text that is difficult to tell apart from human
writing, which threatens academic integrity. Heuristic detectors based on
perplexity, burstiness and stylometry are interpretable but shallow, while
fine-tuned transformer classifiers are accurate but costly and opaque. This
project built \textbf{VeriScript}, an open detector that combines the two in a
confidence-gated cascade: a calibrated heuristic model over 38 features
(GPT-2 token statistics, burstiness, stylometry) decides every document whose
probability lies outside a tuned ambiguity band $[\Tlow,\Thigh]$, and only
borderline documents are escalated to a fine-tuned RoBERTa-base classifier. A
balanced corpus of 44,618 documents was built from HC3, M4, RAID, MAGE and
native/non-native essay sets, with one generator family held out entirely. On
the in-distribution test set the cascade escalated 8\% of documents and reached
F1 0.971, statistically indistinguishable from the transformer (0.975) and well
above the heuristic (0.946), while halving false positives (2.5\% against
5.0\%). The transformer collapsed out of distribution, flagging 69\% of human
MAGE texts and over half of student essays, whereas the cascade stayed far more
robust and explained every fast-path decision with its most influential
features. Two expectations were not met: because the GPT-2 reference model costs
as much as the classifier, the cascade was about twice as slow as the
transformer on CPU, and its native/non-native false-positive disparity was not
lower than the baselines', although its absolute false-positive rates were much
lower than the transformer's. The system is delivered as an open-source library,
command line tool, REST service and web interface, with a reproducible
evaluation framework.
\end{spacing}

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} AI-Generated Text Detection, Hybrid Cascade
Architecture, Perplexity and Burstiness, Transformer Classifiers, Confidence
Gating, Fairness
