# VeriScript — User Manual

*A plain-language guide to what VeriScript is, how it works, and how to use it.*

---

## 1. What is VeriScript?

VeriScript is a free, open-source tool that estimates **whether a piece of English writing was written by a person or generated by an AI chatbot** (such as ChatGPT, GPT-4, Llama or Mistral).

You give it a document — an essay, an assignment, an article — and it tells you:

- **its verdict**: *human-written* or *AI-generated*;
- **how sure it is**: a probability from 0% (certainly human) to 100% (certainly AI);
- **why**: the measurements of the text that influenced the decision most;
- **how it decided**: whether the quick first check was confident enough, or the document was passed to a second, more powerful model.

Everything runs **on your own computer**. Documents are never uploaded to any outside service.

> **Important:** VeriScript gives a *probability*, not proof. No AI detector is perfect. Every result
> should be reviewed by a person before anyone acts on it, and it should **never** be the only basis
> for accusing someone of misconduct. Read section 6 before using it on real students' work.

## 2. Who is it for?

| You are… | VeriScript helps you… |
|---|---|
| A **teacher or examiner** | check essays and assignments, alone or in bulk, and see the reasons behind each score |
| An **institution or IT team** | run a private detection service on your own server, with no per-page fees |
| An **editor or moderator** | flag reviews, comments or articles that may have been mass-produced by AI |
| A **student or researcher** | study how AI-text detection works and how fair and accurate it is |

## 3. How does it work? (no maths)

When a chatbot writes, it picks words that are statistically *likely*. People don't: we make unusual word choices, vary our sentence lengths, and change rhythm. VeriScript looks for these fingerprints in two steps.

**Step 1 — The quick check (the "heuristic" stage).**
VeriScript measures 38 properties of the text, for example:

- **Predictability ("perplexity")** — how easy the text is for a small language model to guess, word by word. AI text tends to be very predictable.
- **Burstiness** — whether sentence lengths vary a lot (human) or are uniform (AI).
- **Vocabulary variety** — how many different words are used.
- **Style** — word length, use of small "function words" (the, of, and…), punctuation, and grammar patterns.

A small machine-learning model turns these measurements into a probability.

**Step 2 — The expert check (the "transformer" stage).**
If the quick check is **confident**, VeriScript answers immediately and shows the measurements that drove the decision. If it is **unsure** (the probability is in a grey zone in the middle), the document is passed to a larger AI model (RoBERTa) that was trained on tens of thousands of examples and reads the whole text in context. Its answer becomes the final one.

```
Your document ──► Quick check ──► confident? ──yes──► Answer + reasons
                                     │
                                     no (grey zone)
                                     ▼
                               Expert check ──────► Answer
```

This two-step design is called a **cascade**. The quick check handles the clear cases; the expert check handles the difficult ones.

## 4. How well does it work?

VeriScript was tested on about 4,000 documents it had never seen (half human, half AI), plus several harder test sets. In plain terms:

- **Accuracy.** In its most careful mode (*thorough*), VeriScript labels about **97 out of 100** documents correctly — the same, statistically, as the expert model used on its own.
- **Fewer false accusations.** Out of 2,000 human-written test documents, VeriScript in *thorough* mode wrongly flagged **49** (2.5%), about **half** as many as either step on its own (94 and 100).
- **Unfamiliar writing.** On writing from new topics and genres, the expert model alone performed badly (it wrongly flagged 69% of human texts in one test, and more than half of student essays). VeriScript's quick check is much more stable, so the combined system is far safer than the expert model alone.
- **New AI models.** When a whole family of AI models (Mistral) was hidden during training, VeriScript still caught 89% of its texts.
- **Fairness.** Like other detectors, VeriScript wrongly flags essays by **non-native English speakers** more often than essays by native speakers (about 1 in 4 TOEFL essays against 1 in 40 native essays in thorough mode). This is a known problem with all such tools; please keep it in mind.
- **Speed.** About 0.07 seconds per document with a graphics card, about 0.36 seconds on an ordinary processor. One planned goal was **not** achieved: the combined system is not faster than the expert model alone, because the quick check itself uses a language model of similar size.

The full results are in the project report (`Project-Report/VeriScript_Final_Report.pdf`, Chapter 7).

## 5. How to use it

### 5.1 Installing (one time)

You need **Python 3.11**. A graphics card (NVIDIA) is optional and only makes it faster.

Open a terminal (PowerShell on Windows) in the project folder and run:

```powershell
py -3.11 -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\python -m pip install torch==2.5.1 --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cpu
.venv\Scripts\python -m pip install -e .
.venv\Scripts\python -m spacy download en_core_web_sm
```

(With an NVIDIA graphics card, use `https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu124` in the second line instead.)

The trained models live in the `models/` folder. If it is empty, follow the "Reproducing the pipeline" section of `README.md` to download the data and train them (this takes a few hours).

### 5.2 The easiest way: the web page

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\veriscript demo
```

Your browser opens a page at `http://localhost:8501`. Then:

1. **Choose a mode** — *fast*, *balanced* or *thorough* (see 5.5).
2. **Paste text** into the box, or switch to **Upload file** and choose a `.txt`, `.pdf` or `.docx` file.
3. Click **Check document**.

You will see the verdict, the probability bar, which route was taken, how long it took, and a table of the most influential measurements, each compared with what is typical for human and for AI writing.

### 5.3 Checking files from the command line

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\veriscript detect essay.docx
.venv\Scripts\veriscript detect --text "Paste the text here..." --mode thorough
```

Example output:

```
ai_generated  p(AI) = 1.000
route: fast_path   latency: 48 ms (heuristic 40, transformer 0)
Decided by the heuristic stage. Most influential features:
  mean log prob = -2.42  (-> AI; human median -3.49, AI median -2.31)
  perplexity = 11.2  (-> AI; human median 32.9, AI median 10.1)
  ...
Probabilistic estimate; review before acting on it.
```

How to read it: the text's *perplexity* is 11.2, much closer to the typical AI value (10.1) than to the typical human value (32.9), so it pushed the decision towards "AI".

### 5.4 Checking many documents at once

Put the files in a folder (or make a spreadsheet saved as CSV with columns `id` and `text`) and run:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\veriscript batch submissions\ --out report.csv --mode thorough
```

`report.csv` opens in Excel and has one row per document (verdict, probability, route, top reasons, notes). A summary file `report.summary.json` gives the totals.

### 5.5 Which mode should I use?

| Mode | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **fast** | Almost always answers with the quick check alone | Quick screening of many documents |
| **balanced** (default) | Sends a very small number of borderline documents to the expert check | Everyday use |
| **thorough** | Sends about 8% of documents (the uncertain ones) to the expert check | When accuracy matters most; recommended for academic decisions |

### 5.6 Running it as a service for others

An IT team can start a private web service that other systems can call:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\veriscript serve
```

Interactive documentation then appears at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/docs`. A Docker image can also be built (see `README.md`).

### 5.7 What the messages mean

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `human_written` / `ai_generated` | The verdict, with the probability of AI authorship |
| `fast_path` | The quick check was confident and decided on its own |
| `escalated` | The quick check was unsure; the expert model decided |
| `insufficient text` | Fewer than 100 words — too short to judge reliably |
| `rejected` | The file could not be read (e.g. a scanned PDF with no text layer) |

## 6. Using VeriScript responsibly

- **A score is not a verdict.** Treat a high score as a reason to look more closely and talk to the author, not as proof.
- **Mind the base rate.** If only a few submissions are actually AI-written, even a 95%-accurate detector produces many false alarms. Expect some flagged documents to be human-written.
- **Be extra careful with non-native English writers**, formal or formulaic writing (technical, legal, very simple prose) and genres the tool has not been trained on (such as school essays). These are flagged more often.
- **Editing and paraphrasing fool detectors.** Text that has been rewritten by a person or a paraphrasing tool is often not detected.
- **English only, 100+ words.** Results on other languages, code, poetry or very short answers are not meaningful.
- **Privacy.** VeriScript keeps documents on your machine and does not save them unless logging is switched on in the configuration file.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Is it free?** Yes. It is open source and uses only openly licensed software and models.

**Does it need the internet?** Only once, to install it and download the models. After that it works offline.

**Can it tell me *which* AI wrote the text?** No. It only estimates whether the text is AI-generated.

**Can it find the AI-written part of a document that is partly human?** No. It judges the document as a whole.

**Can we adapt it to our own students?** Yes. With a set of your own labelled examples, an administrator can retrain the quick check and re-tune the modes:
```powershell
.venv\Scripts\veriscript retrain-heuristic --data our_labelled_examples.csv
.venv\Scripts\veriscript tune --data our_validation_examples.csv
```
